Axle loads default to the selected forklift's library values. Each axle's pressure is load ÷ (number of tires × patch area). Positive lateral shift moves the rear axle's wheels toward the joint edge (from fork.csv, currently 0 for every library truck).
Axle 1 sits against the joint with its trailing edge at the seam; later axles are spaced behind it by their "Gap" (center-to-center). Tire offsets locate each tire right of that axle's left wheel. Each axle's "Lateral Shift" is relative to Axle 1's left wheel (up to 3 axles × 6 tires).
